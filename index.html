The home

Bungalow on a 10,957 sq ft lot in a quiet Coronation Park bay

Welcome to 16 Lipton Place, a spacious detached bungalow nestled in a quiet bay in the desirable Coronation Park neighbourhood. Situated on an impressive 10,957 sq. ft. lot, this well-maintained home offers comfortable living, an abundance of outdoor space, and plenty of flexibility for families, entertaining, or future use.

The main floor features a bright and spacious living room, functional kitchen, dedicated dining area, primary bedroom, two additional bedrooms, and a 4-piece bathroom. The fully finished basement, with its own separate entrance, provides additional living space and versatility, featuring a generous recreation room, a flexible nook ideal for a home office or den, an additional bedroom, 3-piece bathroom, and convenient laundry area.

Step outside and discover the massive, fully fenced backyard, offering exceptional space for gardening, entertaining, relaxing, and enjoying the outdoors. The spacious deck is an ideal spot for summer gatherings and features a built-in BBQ setup, making outdoor cooking and entertaining effortless. With plenty of room for kids, pets, gardening, or creating your own backyard oasis, the outdoor space is truly a standout feature.

Located in a peaceful bay setting within Coronation Park, this home offers a great combination of privacy, space, and convenience. Whether you're looking for a comfortable family home or a property with room to grow and customize, 16 Lipton Place is a property worth seeing!
